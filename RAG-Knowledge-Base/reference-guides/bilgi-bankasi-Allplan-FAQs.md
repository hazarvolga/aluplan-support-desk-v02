# All ALLPLAN FAQs - Your Questions Answered

## 1. General ALLPLAN Subscription FAQ

### What is Subscription?
Subscription is a form of contract whereby you subscribe for a certain period of time to access software and services.

### What do I get with Subscription?
- Access to selected software edition for purchased term
- ALLPLAN Cloud services including BIMPLUS
- Tools for efficient office teamworking
- Teamwork across network and company boundaries
- Management and distribution of drawings
- Market leading product integrations (Solibri Inside, AutoConverter)
- Technical Support teams access
- Online training resources
- New software versions instantly available

### How long do I subscribe for and what if I do not wish to renew?
**SUBSCRIPTION TERMS:**
- Monthly subscription
- Yearly subscription
- Three-year subscription

**CANCELLATION NOTICE PERIODS:**
- Monthly: 1 month notice
- Yearly/3-year: 3 months notice
- All subscriptions auto-renew based on initial term

### How do I pay for my Subscription contract?
**PAYMENT TERMS:** One month, one year, or three years
**PAYMENT METHODS (vary by country):**
- SEPA
- Credit card
- PayPal

Payment applies for entire subscription duration.

### What happens if I cancel my subscription?
- Subscription continues until cancelled
- Automatic loss of access to software and services at end of term

### Do I have any other options to access your software?
**PERPETUAL LICENSE OPTIONS:**
- ALLPLAN Ultimate
- ALLPLAN Civil
- ALLPLAN Precast

**REQUIREMENTS:**
- Mandatory Serviceplus contract for first year
- Renewable thereafter
- Software version at time of purchase + updates for one year
- Does NOT include ALLPLAN Cloud access

### What are the main benefits of subscription?
- Flexibility of approach
- Low fee/cost based on chosen subscription term
- Variable term allowing greater choice and flexibility
- Always benefit from latest software version
- ALLPLAN Cloud services included
- Access to technical support expertise

### How does subscription work?
**SUBSCRIPTION TERMS:** Monthly (ALLPLAN Shop only), yearly, or 3 years
**PAYMENT:** In advance for entire term

**PURCHASE OPTIONS:**
- ALLPLAN Shop: https://shop.allplan.com/en/ALLPLAN/
- ALLPLAN sales contact
- Authorized ALLPLAN Sales Partner

### How can I decide if subscription is the best option for me?
**DECISION TOOLS:**
- 14-day free trial available from all websites
- Monthly subscription option for testing before longer commitment

**SUBSCRIPTION IS GOOD FOR:**
- Flexibility needs
- Low upfront cost preference
- Access to Technical Support
- Always having latest software version
- Maximum productivity benefits

### How can I add more licenses in the future?
**EASY ADDITION OPTIONS:**
- ALLPLAN Shop access
- Contact ALLPLAN sales
- Contact ALLPLAN Authorised Sales Partner
- Available at any time

## 2. ALLPLAN Cloud FAQ

### What cloud storage do I receive with my ALLPLAN Subscription?
- **INCLUDED STORAGE:** 10GB with ALLPLAN Cloud
- **ADDITIONAL STORAGE:** 100GB available for purchase

### What ALLPLAN Cloud services are included?
**CORE SERVICES:**
- Model viewing/management with BIMPLUS
- ALLPLAN Workgroup Manager (office teamworking)
- ALLPLAN Share (cross-network/company teamwork)
- ALLPLAN Exchange (drawing/plan management and distribution)

**INTEGRATIONS:**
- Solibri Inside (model checking)
- AutoConverter (structural analysis tools integration)
- More integrations coming in future releases

### Do you have to use cloud services?
No. Cloud services are available via subscription but usage is optional.

### Is ALLPLAN Workgroup Manager included into ALLPLAN Cloud services?
Yes.

### How will ALLPLAN Workgroup Manager, ALLPLAN Share and ALLPLAN Exchange be made available for all new licenses?
- **EFFECTIVE DATE:** October 1, 2023
- **NEW AVAILABILITY:** Subscription only
- **NO LONGER AVAILABLE:** New perpetual licenses

### Will you still be able to use your existing ALLPLAN Workgroup Manager, Exchange, and Share licenses with perpetual licenses?
**WORKGROUP MANAGER:**
- Compatible with ALLPLAN up to version 2027
- Discontinued Maintenance: October 2027
- NOT compatible with ALLPLAN 2028

**EXCHANGE & SHARE:**
- Discontinued Maintenance: October 2026
- No longer available from October 2026

**FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS:**
- From October 2027: Workgroup Manager new features subscription-only
- From October 2026: Exchange & Share new features subscription-only

### How does subscription apply to BIMPLUS?
- Already included in Subscription license
- Will continue to be available as standalone product

## 3. Serviceplus to Subscription - General FAQ

### What happens when you convert to ALLPLAN Subscription?
**CONVERSION PROCESS:**
- Existing license converted to ALLPLAN Subscription license
- Full access to all ALLPLAN Cloud Services
- Continued access to Technical Support teams
- Continued access to online training resources
- New software versions instantly available

### Do you lose your perpetual license/s?
Yes. When converting to ALLPLAN Subscription, you lose access and rights to your perpetual license/s.

### Do you need to commit to ALLPLAN Subscription for 2 years?
**COMMITMENT TERMS:**
- 12-month subscription contract (for existing SSA/Serviceplus customers)
- ALLPLAN Subscription renewal price fixed for 2 years
- Auto-renewal based on initially chosen term
- 3 months notice required to discontinue

### Do you need to pay the 12-months commitment upfront?
**PAYMENT SCHEDULE:**
- Invoiced every 12 months (annually) before contract start

**PAYMENT TERMS OPTIONS:**
- Bank transfer: 14 days after invoicing
- SEPA direct debit: 30 days after invoicing

### What happens to your existing license/s?
**LICENSE CONVERSION:**
- Existing licenses converted to ALLPLAN Subscription contract licenses
- Surrender perpetual right of usage
- Receive 12-month subscription contract with 2-year price freeze
- Automatically renewed every 12 months (annually)

### Is my purchased ALLPLAN license still my license after version ALLPLAN 2026 and will I be provided with hotfixes?
Yes, existing usage rights and terms and conditions of your existing contracts and licenses will continue to apply.

### Do my ALLPLAN perpetual license products continue to work after End of Life?
**TECHNICAL POSSIBILITY:** Yes, some customers continue using old products

**STRONG RECOMMENDATION:** Do not use unsupported software

**RISKS OF UNSUPPORTED SOFTWARE:**
- Security vulnerabilities
- Stability issues
- Performance problems
- Data compatibility issues

*NOTE: End of Life is common practice in software industry*

### What if I have multiple SSA invoice dates?
**CONTACT OPTIONS:**
- ALLPLAN: https://www.allplan.com/consulting-sale/consulting-sale-overview/
- Local sales partner: https://www.allplan.com/consulting-sale/addresses-sales-partner/
*PURPOSE: Ensure contracts switched at same time*
