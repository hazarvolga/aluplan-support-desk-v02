THE BIM
METHOD
INTEGRAL WORK AND PROCESS OPTIMIZATION
Building Information Modeling (BIM) is actually the “up-to-date”
working method within the building and construction industry
with an integral basic concept. The main focus is on cooperative
collaboration between all involved parties.
With the aim to optimize the whole project lifecycle, a building
model as a “digital twin” of the real project is used to control and
execute the process.
In regard to the project, the use of BIM has a variety of
advantages:

BASIC
REQUIREMENTS
THINK DIFFERENT
The main factors BIM is based on are openness and faithful collaboration between all involved parties. These are fundamental
aspects of successful BIM implementation.
BIM focusses a process oriented approach as well as the relation
and interaction between the single components.
Although a deeper IT experience is helpful, it is not the use of
special “BIM-software” or a high-end workstation that make
the difference.
In fact, a new perspective and a different way of thinking is mostly required instead. This means openness for new experiences.

ADAPT THE WORKFLOW
Traditional project execution is merely design-based, whereas
BIM-compliant project execution is model-oriented.
Similar to the engineers way of thinking, the model is created
first and forms the basis. All drawings(views, details, etc.) and
reports (cost, quantities, etc.) are derived from it.
The BIM focus also means, that people have to adapt their
common workflow, mainly if they haven´t used 3D modeling
features before. This could be a challenge but also the chance for
the more comprehensive use of software.
It is necessary to question accepted routines and to look forward
to new experiences. Only then BIM becomes “live”.

CAD AS A MODELING TOOL
If CAD is used as a 3D modeling tool, the spatial image can be
represented directly on the screen.
Then the mouse becomes the modeling tool while the screen is
the virtual space. Floor plans, views and sections can be derived
directly from the building model. Working in 3D is the first big
step towards the benefits BIM offers.

CAD DIMENSIONS
THE EASY WAY TO BIM
CAD AS A DIGITAL DRAWING BOARD
Working in 2D - many people still use CAD in its elementary form.
Similar to traditional drawing, the mouse is used as a virtual
pencil and the screen represents the paper. In his mind, the
“spectator“ recomposes floor plans, views and sections into a
3D object.

CAD AS A BIM TOOL
Using CAD as a BIM tool means turning a simple 3D body into a
“precise” building component.
The kind of object and also its structure can be defined in detail.
The entire building model is generated in assembling all these
objects into a whole. Besides the derivation of drawings, also a
report and evaluation of the object parameters and quantities is
possible.

BIM 4D – INTERACTION AND TIME:

BIM 5D – PARAMETERS AND COST:

With 4D the time is added to the three spatial dimensions
(X, Y and Z) in assigning the components to the activities of a
time table. Than it is also possible to visualize the construction
procedure and the construction progress in the building model.

As further information, costs are added in 5D BIM to the building
model. The quantities of the single components form the basis
to be linked with cost and prices. In doing so, it is not only possible
to visualize the progress of the construction, but also the course
of the costs of the project.

Even with the use of BIM, the project execution as such stays
the same. It is rather a change in the priorities that implicates a
switch of the effort at the different planing stages.

Due to the extensive interdisciplinary collaboration coordination
is necessarry right from the start. It is based on a project execution plan (PEP), which is part of the contract for all participants.
In the PEP all conditions and specifications as well as the BIM activities/services of everybody involved are defined in detail. That
includes: team responsibilities and coordination tasks, details of
the data exchange procedure, a time schedule for the regular
meetings, in which both a status-quo check and if necessary the
adjustments of the specifications take place.

RESPONSIBILITIES
AWARD ROLES
For efficient collaboration it is important, that everybody involved knows
his or her individual part and responsibility within the entire process. This is
determined right from the start in a detailed service specification. Usually
it forms an hierarchical order.
As in general, also within a BIM project it is important to adapt the organizational structure to the project specifications and also assemble the
team and roles respectively.

BIM Modeler
He or she is the real draftsperson and
creator of the subject-specific model
with all relevant components and parameters. Experience and advanced knowledge in the use of the respective software is necessary for this task.

BIM Coordinator
His or her main task is the internal coordination within a trade or discipline and
therefore he / she is responsible for ensuring a high quality of the related model
or models.

BIM Manager
As the custodian of the client he or she
is responsible for the determination and
implementation of a strategy to reach
the clients target BIM goal. His /her main
task is to coordinate all parties involved
and to ensure the quality of the collaboration model.

The so called level of development (LoD) describes the accuracy and content of information of the single components and the
entire model. It is relevant both for model evaluation and data
exchange.

Common “rules of thumb”, for example that objects with a diameter less than 5 cm should not be modeled in 3D don’t really
make sense.

As it is common practice to “overload” a model with details the
LoD should always be reasonable.

WAYS OF COLLABORATION
CLOSED OR OPEN?
The Open BIM-initiative is an association of leading software vendors from the
building and construction industry. The aim is to enable and enhance a comprehensive
collaboration based on open neutral standards. From a technical point of view IFC and
BCF as software independent data formats are such standards. This leads to a free
choice of the software used out of a broad product portfolio. A growing number of
international open standards ensure the “communication” between different products
also in the long run.

In contrast Closed BIM is a term for the mainly partial use of the
BIM method in the project execution. This limitation primarily
results from the determination, that only software and internal
formats of ONE vendor should be used and thus reduce the
amount of data conversion.

SOFTWARE
TYPES OF BIM TOOLS

BIM is not software at all, but contrary to this software could be
BIM-capable and thus be used to implement and apply a BIM
compliant workflow. In this respect, a distinction is drawn between three kinds of software. Usually they are used in interaction with each other:

>	Modeling tools: Software both to create data and
information and to analyze it by using integrated functions
>	Coordination tools: Software to coordinate data with
additional functions to check and collaborate it
>	Analysis tools: Software to analyze the model data. They
can also be integrated into the modeling tools as separate
features
