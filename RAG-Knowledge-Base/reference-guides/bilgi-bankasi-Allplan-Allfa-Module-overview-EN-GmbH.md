ALLPLAN ALLFA
MODULE OVERVIEW
Allplan Allfa is a browser-based CAFM solution that meets highest demands in infrastructural and
technical facilities management. The software has a wide array of individualy selectable specialist
modules for the different aspects of building management. Thereby you cover all upcoming tasks
with a single solution. Due to comprehensive evaluation functions and a central database along with
an integrated history, Allplan Allfa is the ideal tool for monitoring data and costs as well as fulfilling the
legal requirements.

ALLPLAN ALLFA – THE BASIS
ALLPLAN ALLFA WEB

Web-based access to Allplan Allfa
> Location-independent access to the Allplan Allfa database via intranet and internet
> Graphical display and evaluation of information as well as online search, incl. reporting
> Creation of structures or editing of existing structures and data
> Browser support, no local installation required
> Efficient management of all property-, building- and facility-related information
> Management and documentation of the organisational structure, surfaces, spaces, use,
equipment, staff
> Simple documentation of relevant data for building ecology such as wall construction
> Capturing of not clearly assigned spatial systems such as heating, air-conditioning and
fire alarm systems

ALLPLAN ALLFA TT

The working environment for admins
> Structured and standardized data management via catalogs
> Object-oriented selection and display of data
> Complementing additional information such as cost centers, cost distribution or substraction areas
used in cleaning cost calculation
> Execution of bulk-data operations
> Database interface ADE for simple data import and export

EXTENSIONS
ALLFA WEB REPORT

The module for professional web-based report creation
> Create and manage reports and analyses user-friendly
> Web-based creating and layouting of report templates
> Integrated scheduler for timebased, automated planning and sending of reports
> Create real-time reports via ad hoc reporting
> Web-based repository for centralised storage of reports and templates
> Detailed access rights for reports

ALLFA FORM
DESIGNER

The module for creating and editing the Allplan Allfa interface
> Design Allplan Allfa according to your requirements
> Ease of use via drag&drop
> Allfa Form Designer is available as a web solution and therefore requires no local installation
> Comprehensive user and version concept
> Create customized views of your data and make them available for selected groups,
based on user privileges

ALLFA PROCESS

The workflow module for Allplan Allfa
> Mapping of customer-specific work processes with access to stored data
> Direct access to the Allplan Allfa database via the internet
> High degree of adaptability thanks to graphical editor
> Comprehensive user and version concept
> Allfa Process is available as a web solution and does not have to be installed locally
> Scope of delivery: Web application including a standard service workflow
> Ideal for items like a central helpdesk or maintenance & service management
> Allfa Process contains the module Allfa Form Designer

ALLFA INTELLIADMIN

Administration of users, user groups and user rights
> Simple creation of users
> Definition and creation of detailed user groups and corresponding user rights
> Clear limits for viewing and editing rights
> Storage and management of access rights in the database
> Optimized protection against unauthorized access to data, across the system and across modules
> Assignment of rights down to field level

ALLFA DOCUMENTS

The IT-supported archiving system
> Management of plans and documents in tamper-proof PDF/A format
> Access assignment and control of access rights through integration in the Allplan Allfa database
> Clear document status based on CheckOut/CheckIn function and different processing status
> Clear classification due to site log book structure, comprehensive search function and
keyword catalog
> Allocation of several files to one document

ALLFA PLUS

The comfort package for Allplan Allfa
> Comprehensive catalog for basic functions, e.g. risk symbols, IT equipment, furniture, technical
facility symbols and electrical symbols
> Predefined reports for queries based on e.g. floor cleaning area, cleaning type and cycle of rooms and
area distribution
> Management of cleaning services
> Filter for database queries, e.g. by rooms without occupancy type, frequency of characteristics
used in the catalog or search by text patterns in the label field

ALLFA VARIANTS

Planning variants and history
> Optional extension
> Display of former phases in the buildings and their substructures depending on time and space
> Management of individual, flexible, freely-definable time phase formation and changes
> Retrospective overview of companies, departments, employees, technical structures and
cost centers
> Storage of historical data supports forecast and planning
> Better basis for decision-making in company restructuring
> Support for relocation lists, analysis of new acquisitions, disposal lists, updating of telephone lists
and address information

SPECIALIZED MODULES
ALLFA WEB INFO

Web-based working environment
> Read-only access to Allplan Allfa database via intranet and internet
> Graphical display and online search, incl. reportings
> Browser support, no local installation required
> The optimal information solution for your company

ALLFA CLEANING

The integrated solution for maintenance cleaning
> Optional extension
> Cleaning categories incl. services and frequency
> Fast definition of cleaning thanks to bulk update functions
> Creation and management of cleaning contracts
> Creation of service specifications for tenders
> Monitoring of costs
> Pro-rata distribution of cleaning costs

ALLFA INVENTORY

Collection and inventory with barcode scanner
> Combined hardware and software solution, integrated in Allplan Allfa
> Identification of recorded objects on initial inclusion, inventory, or documentation of checks
> Automatic assignment of newly recorded equipment to the corresponding rooms possible
> Entry of equipment status
> Comparison and check of recorded data for new or missing equipment
> Inventory based on separate article catalog available in Allplan Allfa
> Easy and customized addition of input fields for expanding the input range

ALLFA KEYS

Key management
> Solution integrated in Allplan Allfa for the management and storage of keys and locks
> Structured work processes for key issue and return, as well as lock installation and removal
> Automatic documentation of work processes for comprehensive administration (history)
> Numerous analyses and reporting functions
> Overview of employee access rights (access administration)
> Management of key plans by mapping relationships between employee, room, door lock and key

INTERFACES
ALLFA SYNC FOR
ALLPLAN

The interface for bidirectional data exchange
> Attribute assignment for rooms and equipment, as well as occupancy and employees
in Allplan Architecture
> Input of any Allplan Architecture attributes
> Access to special FM lists within the Allplan Architecture schedules
> Import and export of data in XLS format
> Use of Allplan Allfa structures and data via offline and online catalogs
> Assignment of equipment of all types and for all occupancies
> Bidirectional replication of Allplan Architecture data with the Allplan Allfa database

ALLFA SYNC FOR
AUTOCAD

The interface for bidirectional data exchange
> Attribute assignment for equipment and rooms in AutoCAD and AutoCAD Architecture
> Assignment already in the planning phase
> Creation of intelligent objects with assignment in the building hierarchy
> Analysis of room polygons, blocks and Xrefs
> Bidirectional replication of AutoCAD data with the Allplan Allfa database

ALLFA REAL
INTERFACE

The SAP interface
> Flexible configuration based on your requirements and the modules used
> Connection possible e.g. to SAP R/3 modules CO (Controlling), FI (Financial Accounting),
HR (Human Resources), PM (Plant Maintenance), RE (Real Estate)
> Direct access to SAP data
> Strict data separation thanks to predefined read and write authorizations
> Automated processes enable high-quality, up-to-date data
> Optional direct information service on a defined key date

ALLFA MAXIMO

Maintenance, warehouse and resource management
> Mapping of all processes in maintenance, from order management during procurement,
maintenance and inspection through to disposal of systems
> Integrated workflow enables automation of maintenance processes
> Fully web browser-based client application that does not require additional installations
> Integration of the system in the existing Allplan Allfa environment
> Automated replication of all relevant objects such as building, equipment, catalogs and
personnel data
Connection to LDAP User Administration
> Online verification of LDAP users
> Import of LDAP structures
> Integrated in the Allplan Allfa Admin module
