BFU RELIES ON ALLPLAN PRECAST
The efficiency gains provided by the automated shop
drawing technology are one of the many reasons why
BFU has long relied on Allplan Precast. The software
was also used for the design and detailing of the
precast elements for the Rütihof commercial building.
Within just nine months, BFU designed and produced
all precast elements on time and with the highest
quality.
