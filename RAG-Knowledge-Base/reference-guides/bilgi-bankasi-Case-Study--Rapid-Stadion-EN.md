SUSTAINABLE CONSTRUCTION
The precast technology also scores in terms of
sustainability: during construction of the stadion,
energy consumption was reduced by 60% thanks
to the precast design. Due to precise planning and
better waste management, the amount of waste was
reduced by 90% compared to a traditional construction method.
