CLOUD-BASED LICENSING - MESSAGE: ‘LICENCE USAGE FAILED`



Question:
I receive the message ‘Licence usage failed’ or 'local license expired" in ALLPLAN.
What does this mean and what do I need to do?
 
 
Response:
The messages indicate that the network connection is not stable and/or there are problems with access rights. The cause of the interruption may also be the router or an unstable WLAN connection (if no LAN cable is used). The virus scanner may also be causing the problem in connection with real-time scanning.
 
1. check the firewall settings
The following authorisations are required licence.allplan.com, login.allplan.com, connect.allplan.com, portal.bimplus.net and connect2.allplan.com. 
Port 443/HTTPS must be enabled for all the addresses mentioned.
 
2. check whether access is blocked by the virus scanner
Real time scanner blocks Allplan data
 
3. general problems with Internet access
Have your IT department check whether there are any general problems with Internet access.
