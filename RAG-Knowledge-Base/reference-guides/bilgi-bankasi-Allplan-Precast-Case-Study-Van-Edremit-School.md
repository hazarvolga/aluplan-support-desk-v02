# BIM in Practice: Art and Precast Elements in Turkey - The Van Edremit School

## Project Overview
The art school in Edremit, Turkey, offers young artists a total area of 5,000 square meters. The building accommodates various workshops for painting, sculpting, and ceramics, alongside a large 275-person conference room and an expansive exhibition hall. Generous windows ensure ideal daylight conditions. However, the architectural ambitions required massive dimensions of windows and filigree slabs, making the planning of the precast elements highly challenging. 

The design team from **ETE İNŞAAT SAN.TİC LTD.ŞTİ** utilized **Allplan Precast** to overcome these hurdles with flying colors.

### Project Information at a Glance
- **Focus:** Precast Concrete
- **Software for Precast Design:** Allplan Precast
- **Design and Production:** ETE İNŞAAT SAN.TİC LTD.ŞTİ
- **Number of Elements:** 600 double walls, 300 element slabs, 60 precast beams
- **Precast Planning:** 15 days
- **Production Time:** 45 days
- **Assembly Time:** 45 days

## Shop Drawings and Efficiency
The ETE team, who has relied on Allplan for over 13 years, heavily utilized the software to create accurate 3D models. The software successfully coordinates MEP systems (electrical/mechanical) and automatically generates rapid shop drawings. This generated drawing package seamlessly integrates reinforcement, fixtures, and dimensioning automatically, keeping details correct and reliable for production.

### Automatic Elementation for 600 Double Walls
The technical team benefited immensely from intelligent automatic elementation in *Allplan Precast Slabs*. The software automatically divided wall surfaces and floor slabs into precast units that match factory production variables. The software actively considers the production constraints of the factory side by side with the load-bearing capacity of the construction site crane. For this specific project, this function allowed identical and rapid partitioning of 600 complex double walls.

### Highly Custom Individual Reinforcement
Despite automatic generation routines, ETE maintained total control over bespoke reinforcement adjustments. The placement of the rebars remained highly flexible, adapting perfectly to local demands while managing 300 filigree slabs smoothly.

## Earthquake Safety
Because the region in Van, Turkey, is an earthquake-prone zone, structurally resistant construction is absolutely critical. Allplan Precast enabled the engineers to incorporate high seismic thresholds from the very beginning of the BIM modeling stage. Since every step (design, detailing, and shop drawings) occurred strictly within Allplan, adherence to regional safety regulations was guaranteed. 

### Seamless Factory Production
The drawing and 3D data transfer utilized **MES Connect**, seamlessly feeding precise geometry and required materials directly into the production machines.
