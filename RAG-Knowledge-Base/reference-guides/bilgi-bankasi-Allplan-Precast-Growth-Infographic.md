# Growth Opportunity for Prefabrication
**The Global Precast Concrete Market** is set to grow by 40% from USD $111.21 billion to USD $156.13 billion between 2024 and 2030 (Source: Grand View Research).

## Factors for a Shift Towards Prefabrication
The numerous advantages of prefabrication – such as cost-effectiveness, reliable scheduling, and high production quality – are accelerating the rapid growth of the precast industry.

- Demanding quality requirements for products
- Rising costs and tighter time constraints
- Continuing shortage of skilled workers
- Stricter safety and welfare standards
- Increasing sustainability and environmental protection requirements
