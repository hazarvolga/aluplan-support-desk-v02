# BIM in Practice: Architectural Freedom Meets Highest Engineering Art - Identity Project
**INCLINED WALLS MADE OF PRECAST ELEMENTS THANKS TO ALLPLAN PRECAST WALLS**

## Overview
The "Identity" building complex in Rennes, France, is an extraordinary architectural masterpiece. It was designed by the architectural firm Blanchard Marsault Pondevie as a crystal geode formed by three interconnected rock sections. 

A silver mesh made from aluminium envelops the three building parts and connects the roofs and facades. The three buildings ("Identity 1, 2, and 3") form the modern EuroRennes business district near the main train station, housing offices, stores, and a cinema.

## Design Flexibility and The Challenge
One of the most complex structural features of this project are the **inclined walls**, which give the building its unique crystalline appearance. The French precast company **Société de Préfabrication de Landaul (SPL)**, a specialist in precast walls, was responsible for designing and manufacturing these highly customized, inclined concrete walls for "Identity 2".

This represented a first for SPL; it was the first time in the company's history that such aggressively inclined precast walls were planned and produced.

### Project Information at a Glance
- **Focus:** Precast construction
- **Location:** Rennes, France
- **Software:** Allplan Precast Walls
- **Precast Designer:** Société de Préfabrication de Landaul (SPL)
- **Architecture Firm:** Blanchard Marsault Pondevie
- **Inclined Precast Area:** 800m² of inclined walls
- **Floors Above Ground:** 9
- **Total Office Space:** 7,934m²
- **Project Duration:** One year from design to construction

## Automated Workflows and Delivery
For precast planning, SPL relied entirely on its long-time partner Allplan Precast Walls. Allplan Precast Walls enables the highly automated design and production of customized precast elements. For this unique task, the design team utilized the perfect mix of automated workflows and individual customization to adapt to the challenging geometries.

SPL takes immense pride in manufacturing all precast elements to the highest quality criteria while maintaining fixed prices and strict delivery dates. The entire project duration from planning the complex inclined walls to final installation was just one year.

## About SPL
SPL (Société de Préfabrication de Landaul) is located in the northwest of France. Founded in 2008, the company employs around 80 people and produces approximately 270,000 m² of precast concrete walls annually using two state-of-the-art precast factories and dual design offices.
