CLOUD LIZENZIERUNG - BIMPLUS-MIGRATION INTO CLOUD-BASED LICENSING OF ALLPLAN



New ALLPLAN Cloud Licensing for Bimplus, Share, and Other ALLPLAN Cloud Services 
The ALLPLAN Cloud Services are following the general transition of our products to the new cloud-based licensing solution. As of April 2025, the old licensing method will no longer be recognized, and the associated Allplan Cloud licenses will be managed via Connect. There is a limited transition period during which we will ensure that you can still use the products even without a correctly assigned license. 
Products affected are: ALLPLAN Bimplus, ALLPLAN Share, ALLPLAN COR, ALLPLAN Exchange.
 All ALLPLAN Cloud licenses are named licenses and can only be assigned to a specific user via reservations. They are globally valid and personally assigned by name. In order to continue working with the affected products, users must be invited to the license organization in Connect and be assigned the relevant licenses.
The ALLPLAN Cloud / Bimplus license is valid for every team and every project to which the user is invited.
 If you are a user: Please contact your company’s license administrator so that they can assign/reserve a license for you.
 If you are a license administrator (with access to the Manage section in Connect): In general, license management is now unified for both ALLPLAN licenses and ALLPLAN Cloud licenses and is handled through ALLPLAN Connect. If you are not familiar with this concept, the links provided can help, or you can use the built‑in onboarding feature in the lower right corner of ALLPLAN Connect via the rocket icon. 
Licensing Pages on ALLPLAN Help (Link to localized version: https://help.allplan.com/Allplan/2025-0/1033/License/index.htm#427799.htm)
FAQ on Licensing on Learn Now (EN): (Link: https://learnnow.allplan.com/totara/engage/resources/howto/index.php?id=7453&source=howto)

Change of Bimplus Licenses
When a user is invited to a project and does not have a license, they will receive the selected role. However, without a license, they cannot perform the tasks associated with that role and will instead only have permissions similar to a viewer until a license is assigned to them. The invitation dialog explains this to the inviting administrator:
NOTE: To ensure that the invited user can fully participate in your project/team, please make sure they have the appropriate license. Without a valid license, their access is restricted and they may not be able to make an active contribution. If the user requires a license, please contact your organization’s administrator to assign one.
After the invited user has accepted the invitation from Connect, you, as the company’s license administrator, must assign a license to the user. From that moment on, they can fully carry out their role. 
To make your transition from team-based licenses easier, you can, for a limited time, initiate the process from the Connect Manage Dashboard, which will guide you through the steps and provide an overview of all Bimplus team members from Bimplus teams belonging to your company who require a license.
 This procedure is not mandatory, since the same result can also be achieved through manual license administration — but it is strongly recommended to save time and effort.
 Through this process, the cloud licenses are grouped into a new entitlement. Selected users are divided into two new user groups:
	•	one for Bimplus users within your company
	•	one for external persons invited to your Bimplus teams
Licenses are then automatically assigned and reserved for these users.
 During the process, you can determine for each user whether they should have access to a cloud license (covering all ALLPLAN Cloud products) or a Bimplus Pro license, depending on which licenses are available in your company.
Note:
	•	Internal users are linked to your company.
	•	External users are not (yet) linked to your company. It is always possible to manually move people between user groups after the process is completed if needed.
 Since users can belong to multiple user groups, this procedure only affects the use of ALLPLAN Cloud licenses. The use of ALLPLAN licenses and entitlements, as well as existing user groups, remains unaffected.
