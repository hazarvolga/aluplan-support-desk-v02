# Allplan Precast Fabrication: For Precast Designers Who Demand More

Precast is one of the fastest-growing segments of the construction industry. That's why ALLPLAN offers unique workflows for the design, detailing, and implementation of precast projects. Highly automated workflows enable maximum productivity and precision.

## End-to-End Solutions for Prefabrication
What characterizes successful precast projects is the perfect mix of extensive knowledge, the commitment to absolute precision, and the right tools. Allplan supports you across all project phases:
- Impressive 3D quotation models
- Highly accurate 3D precast models
- Complete sequence and production planning
- Clear 3D logistics/stacking
- Seamless billing data

## Key Advantages
- **TIME SAVING (52%):** Faster and more accurate design deliverables, visualizations, and detailed construction drawings compared to 2D methods.
- **LESS ERRORS (60%):** More efficient and precise project costing at earlier stages with reliable automated quantities and materials take-offs.
- **MORE PRODUCTIVE (25%):** Increased overall productivity with efficient 3D stacking solutions.
