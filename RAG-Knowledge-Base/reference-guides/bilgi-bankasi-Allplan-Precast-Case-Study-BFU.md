# BIM in Practice on the Rütihof Project
**How ALLPLAN Precast Enables Efficient Collaboration**

The Rütihof project in Switzerland is a perfect example of the successful implementation of the openBIM concept. From the very beginning, the two project partners, Beton-Fertigteil-Union (BFU) and METHABAU, relied on BIM.

## Project Information at a Glance
- **Focus:** Precast construction
- **Precast Design Software:** Allplan Precast
- **Precast Design:** BFU
- **Main Contractor:** METHABAU
- **Construction Period:** June 2019 - March 2020
- **Produced Precast Elements:** 10,000m² of slabs, 1,100m² of walls, ~50 stair elements

## The Project Scope and Collaboration
BFU was responsible for the design, detailing, and production of the precast elements. The Swiss company METHABAU acted as the main contractor, taking over the project development, the complete design in 3D and BIM, the execution planning, as well as the construction services.

The key to their joint success was efficient collaboration between all parties involved in the construction project – and this is where openBIM came in.

### Smooth Information Flows via IFC
Previously, the time-consuming design process involved METHABAU drafting paper plans in 2D and submitting them to BFU. Then, BFU would draw shop drawings and send them back to METHABAU for checking. 

This time-consuming and error-prone process is now a thing of the past. Today, data exchange is optimized via **IFC** (Industry Foundation Classes). With the help of the IFC-Assistant and the MEP-Assistant, BFU was able to quickly implement the elements in Allplan Precast, obtaining high-quality production plans and shop drawings. Allplan Precast proved its value through the highly automated design and production of slabs and walls.

### Modeling in Record Time
Thanks to the automated workflows in Allplan Precast, significant time savings in design and detailing were achieved. OpenBIM—software-independent data exchange—was integrated and made a reality. From the beginning of the construction project to its completion, all relevant data and models were consolidated and available at any time for all participants. 

This common BIM model considerably reduced the effort required to coordinate works. Thanks to standardized workflows and templates, all parties involved gained more clarity. Any collisions and modeling errors were reliably detected when the specialist models were merged.

The unique automated shop drawing technology in Allplan Precast provided both the clarity and vividness of the 3D model, alongside the simplicity of the 2D plan.

## BFU Relies on ALLPLAN Precast
The efficiency gains provided by the automated shop drawing technology are one of the many reasons why BFU has long relied on Allplan Precast. Within just nine months, BFU designed and produced all precast elements for the Rütihof commercial building on time and with the highest quality.

## About BFU (Beton-Fertigteil-Union GmbH & Co. KG)
BFU is a medium-sized company in Baden-Württemberg, Germany that manufactures a wide range of precast concrete parts. Their product portfolio ranges from walls and slabs to structural precast elements such as balconies, stairs, or facades, including architectural concrete.
